Legal Disclaimer

In their current form, AI models will not always produce an accurate output. In fact, they may not produce the same output from two identical inputs. Furthermore, this tool should never be used as legal advice.

Further, this tool should be used for educational purposes. If you do plan to use a generated letter, ensure that you read it carefully and check for its accuracy, as it cannot be guaranteed.

This HIPAA communication tool generator produces customizable formal letters and provider-interaction tips across treatment settings and needs; the framework originated from extensive input by our peer and family advisory committees. Committee members identified persistent challenges navigating HIPAA barriers and a clear preference for interactive, personalized tools over static PDFs. Drawing from concrete examples supplied by members, we developed a structured system prompt that was iteratively refined through multiple rounds of testing across AI platforms, placeholder combinations, and leading prompting styles. The final system prompt incorporates essential fields (caregiver and patient names, relationship, core pathway, treatment setting, provider and caregiver roles, information needed, action sought, challenges encountered, and information to be shared) and precise instructions that guide framing, tone, and content for the outputted formal letter and communication tips.

The original design intended the system prompt to remain invisible and be submitted via API after form entry. Model performance proved highly variable, however, leading to a pivot: users now receive a ready-to-use system prompt that they copy into the AI platform of their choice. This approach preserves flexibility as AI capabilities evolve. At present we recommend Claude for its strong letter-generation performance; future models may surpass it, yet the underlying prompt remains stable, so outputs continue to improve. The entire development approach is grounded in lived expertise and spans conception, iterative development, testing, and publication. Subsequent versions will explore further customization to deliver even more timely, robust, and compassionate advocacy tools.
